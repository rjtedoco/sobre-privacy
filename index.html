Sobre Privacy Policy

Effective: 3 October 2026

Short version: it's on your phone. Nothing leaves it unless you choose to turn something on.

Sobre is a balance tracker for your accounts, bills and debts. There is no Sobre account, no sign-up and no login. Your numbers live in a database on your phone.

What we collect

Nothing, by default. Sobre does not collect your name, email, phone number, contacts, location, balances, bills, debts or any other personal information. We never receive it, so we can't read it, sell it or lose it.

The only thing the app asks our server for at launch is its settings (a plain request that carries none of your data). Like any internet request, it reaches us with an IP address, which we don't use to identify or track you.

Optional: anonymous usage

The first time you have an account in Sobre, we ask whether you'll share anonymous usage. The default is off, and "No" is a fine answer. You can change it any time in You > Share anonymous usage.

If you say yes, Sobre sends small events about how the app is used: which screens you open, when you add an account, bill or debt, log a payment, update a balance, use the what-if, switch the debt strategy, export or delete your data, or turn app lock, backup or reminders on or off. Once a day it also sends a rough shape of your setup: how many accounts, bills and debts you have (as ranges like "1-3"), which kinds of account and debt you have, whether you keep any money set aside, how recently you updated a balance and how many of your balances are confirmed (also as ranges), plus your text-size setting, Reduce Motion and whether notifications are allowed. Each event carries only a count range, a yes/no, or a fixed label. Never amounts or balances in any form, never account, bank, wallet or bill names, merchants, anything you type, or anything derived from your money. Every event also carries basic technical details of your phone and the app: device model, iOS version, app version and build, language and region setting, time zone and screen size. Events are tagged with a random ID that isn't linked to you, is replaced with a new one each time you opt in, and is deleted when you opt out. PostHog receives your phone's IP address to deliver each request, but we've set it to discard the IP instead of storing it, and we switch off location lookup, so no location is derived. We don't read your advertising ID.

If the app crashes, it can also send a crash report: the type of error and where in the code it happened, with the error message and any text stripped out first. It carries the same random ID and no personal content.

We use this to learn which parts of Sobre get used and where the app breaks so we can make it better. Turn the switch off and sending stops immediately. The events are handled by PostHog (us.i.posthog.com), our analytics provider.

iCloud backup

If you turn on backup, Sobre makes an encrypted copy of your data (AES-256) and puts it in your own iCloud Drive. The key lives in your iCloud Keychain. Apple stores both; we never see the backup, the key or what's inside. That also means we can't recover it for you: it's yours, and so is the responsibility to keep your recovery code safe.

On your phone

  • App lock and Face ID / Touch ID: handled by iOS. Sobre only learns "unlocked or not", never your biometrics.
  • Bill and debt reminders: scheduled on your phone. Sobre doesn't use push notifications.
  • Photos and files: Sobre only touches a screenshot or file you pick. Reading it happens on your phone.
  • Statement and screenshot reading (the extraction service) is not used in this version.

Your rights, and your data

It's your data. You can:

  • Export all of it from You > Export my data (a CSV or JSON file you can share or save).
  • Delete all of it from You > Delete all my data. This wipes the database on your phone. Any iCloud backup is yours to manage in iCloud Drive.
  • Turn off analytics at any time.

Because we hold no personal data about you, there is nothing for us to export or delete on our side. If you shared anonymous usage and want it gone, tell us (contact below) and we'll delete what we can match to you, though the ID is random and may not be traceable.

If you're in the Philippines, the Data Privacy Act of 2012 (RA 10173) gives you rights to be informed, to access, correct and erase your data, and to complain to the National Privacy Commission (privacy.gov.ph). Wherever you live, you can ask us what we hold, and we'll tell you honestly. Usually: nothing.

Children

Sobre isn't made for children under 13 and we don't knowingly collect anything from them. If you think a child has shared data through analytics, contact us and we'll delete it.

Changes

If this changes in a way that matters (for example, a new feature that sends data off your phone), we'll update this page and ask you in the app before anything is sent.
